Medical billing is the process of creating and submitting claims to insurance companies for healthcare services provided to patients. 
It ensures that healthcare providers receive payment for their services. 
The billing process includes charge entry, claim submission, and follow-up. 
Accurate billing requires proper patient information, insurance details, and medical codes. 
Errors in billing can lead to claim denials or payment delays. 
Billing teams work closely with coding and insurance teams to ensure correct reimbursement. 
Timely billing improves cash flow and financial performance of healthcare organizations.
