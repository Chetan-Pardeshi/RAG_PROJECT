Eligibility verification is the process of checking whether a patient’s insurance coverage is active. 
It is done before providing medical serv    ices to avoid claim rejections. 
The process includes verifying patient details, policy status, and coverage benefits. 
It ensures that the services are covered under the insurance plan. 
Incorrect eligibility verification can result in denied claims or unpaid bills. 
Healthcare staff use online portals or contact insurance providers to verify eligibility. 
This step is important for smooth claim processing and payment.
