Diabetes is a chronic disease that occurs when the body cannot properly regulate blood sugar levels. It happens when the pancreas does not produce enough insulin or when the body cannot effectively use the insulin it produces.

Common symptoms of diabetes include frequent urination, increased thirst, extreme hunger, fatigue, and blurred vision. Maintaining a healthy diet and regular exercise can help manage diabetes.

Hypertension, also known as high blood pressure, is a condition where the force of blood against the artery walls is too high. It can lead to serious health problems such as heart disease and stroke.

Lifestyle changes such as regular physical activity, reducing salt intake, maintaining a healthy weight, and avoiding smoking can help prevent hypertension.

Asthma is a chronic respiratory condition that affects the airways in the lungs. During an asthma attack, the airways become inflamed and narrow, making it difficult to breathe.

Common asthma triggers include pollen, dust mites, air pollution, cold air, and respiratory infections. Asthma can be managed with medication and avoiding triggers.

A healthy lifestyle includes balanced nutrition, regular exercise, adequate sleep, and stress management. These habits help reduce the risk of many diseases and improve overall health.
